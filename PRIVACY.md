# Privacy

Iris is a browser made by Tulipan Labs. This page says what it does with your data.

**Iris collects nothing.** There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash
reporting. Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to Tulipan Labs or to anyone on its
behalf. Tulipan Labs runs no servers for Iris.

## What stays on your device

- History, settings, the open tabs, cookies, sign-ins and cache are kept in your user
  profile. Each container keeps its own cookies, storage and cache, apart from the others.
  Private tabs keep nothing after the last one closes.
- Files you download go to your Downloads folder.

## What goes out, and to whom

- **The sites you open** receive what any browser sends them. Their own policies apply.
- **Google**, when you search: what you typed goes to Google AI Mode, with Iris's short
  instruction to answer from high-quality sources.
- **GitHub**, once a day: Iris asks for the newest release, to tell you about updates (and,
  on Android, to download one when you say so). The request carries nothing about you
  beyond your network address.
- **The web engine** (Chromium, through CEF, on Linux; Chromium, through Cefrium, on Android,
  and Android System WebView for the sandbox there) follows its maker's own privacy terms.
