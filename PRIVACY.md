# Privacy

Iris is a browser made by Tulipan Labs. This page says what it does with your data.

**Iris collects nothing.** There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash
reporting. Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to Tulipan Labs or to anyone on its
behalf. Tulipan Labs runs no servers for Iris.

## What stays on your computer

- History, settings, sign-ins, cookies and cache are kept in your user profile on this
  computer. Private, temporary and sandbox tabs keep none of it after they close.
- Adaptive shields remember, per site, whether anything needed blocking. Private tabs leave
  no record.
- Files you download, or receive from your phone, go to your Downloads folder.

## What goes out, and to whom

- **The sites you open** receive what any browser sends them. Their own policies apply.
- **Google**, when you search or use Ask AI: your query goes to Google, and with Ask AI the
  text of the page (or your selection) goes with your question. Suggestions while you type
  come from your own history and are not sent anywhere.
- **Filter lists** (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters) are downloaded from their
  publishers every few days. The request carries nothing about you beyond your network
  address.
- **The phone link** serves one page on your own network while it is on, reachable only
  with the key in its QR code. Files move between your phone and this computer inside that
  network and never pass through the internet. The connection is not encrypted.
- **The web engine** (WebKitGTK on Linux; Chromium through Cefrium on Android) is part of
  your system or of Iris, and follows its maker's own privacy terms.
