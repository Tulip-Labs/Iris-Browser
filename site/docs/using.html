Using Iris

This page covers Iris for Linux. Iris has no main menu: everything is a shortcut, a button in the chrome, the right-click menu or the command palette. Items marked From 2.3 arrive with Iris 2.3.

Search and the address bar

Ctrl+L or F6 puts the keyboard in the address bar. Type an address, or anything else to search Google. Each suggestion list also offers Ask Google AI Mode. Start with yt to search YouTube: yt cats.

The start page From 2.3 is drawn by Iris itself, with no web page behind it. A new tab opens at once and uses no web process until you go somewhere. Alt+Home takes the tab back to it.

Tabs

  • Ctrl+T opens a new tab and Ctrl+W closes one. Ctrl+Shift+T brings closed tabs back where they were, pinned or not.
  • Pinned tabs From 2.3: choose Pin Tab from a tab's menu. A pinned tab stays at the left as its icon.
  • Sound From 2.3: a tab playing sound shows a speaker. Click it, or press Ctrl+M, to mute.
  • Duplicate From 2.3: Ctrl+Shift+K.
  • Links that open a new window open as a tab next to yours. Script pop-ups need your click and may only go to the same site or a sign-in page; anything else is stopped, with an Open button if you meant it.
  • Every tab stays awake: Iris has no sleeping tabs. Closing a tab ends its page and frees its memory.

Workspaces From 2.3

Workspaces are named sets of tabs. Make one from the Containers menu or the command palette, and switch with Alt+1…9. The first workspace is Home; removing a workspace moves its tabs there. Every tab and workspace comes back next time.

Split View From 2.3

Ctrl+Shift+S shows two tabs side by side. Drag the divider between them to resize.

Compact mode From 2.3

Ctrl+Shift+C hides the tabs and the bar. They slide down in glass when the pointer reaches the top of the window.

Peek From 2.3

Alt+click a link, or choose Peek Link from its menu, to open it in a floating glass card over the page.

  • Esc or a click beside the card closes it, and its view and process go with it.
  • Enter, Ctrl+Enter or the expand button turns it into a tab.

Reader From 2.3

On an article, a Reader button appears in the address bar. Click it or press Ctrl+Alt+R to see the article alone, on a calm glass page in light or dark that runs no script.

Command palette From 2.3

Ctrl+K opens the palette. It finds your tabs in every workspace, Iris's commands and your history by a fuzzy match. Settings such as HTTPS first and Shields for the current site can be switched from here too.

Find and zoom

  • Ctrl+F finds in the page.
  • Ctrl++, Ctrl+− and Ctrl+0 zoom in, zoom out and reset.
  • F11 is full screen. Videos can go full screen too.

Downloads

Files go to your Downloads folder and show in a glass panel with their progress (Ctrl+Shift+Y or Ctrl+J). The downloads button appears while a file comes in, then shows how many finished until you open it. Downloaded files get safe names.

Containers

Containers keep sites apart. Iris starts with none: you make your own, and each paints its tabs in its colour.

  • Each container keeps its own cookies, storage, cache and sign-ins, on disk.
  • Open a new tab in a container, or open the current page in another one.
  • Removing a container deletes everything its sites kept.

Private tabs and the sandbox

Private tabs (Ctrl+Shift+N) share one in-memory world, which is gone when the last private tab closes.

The sandbox is automatic. Sites Iris does not trust open in it without asking: plain http, a bare IP address (in any form) or a failing certificate.

  • It has web processes of its own and a network session per tab, in memory only.
  • It gives none of your data to the site, and allows no downloads, camera, microphone, location, clipboard, WebGL, WebRTC or pop-ups.
  • Sandboxed tabs have an orange rim.

HTTPS first From 2.3

On by default: an http address is tried over https first. When a site has no https, one click takes you back to http, which opens in the sandbox. Turn it off in the Shields panel or from the command palette.

Forget everything on close From 2.3

Off by default. Turn it on in the Shields panel. When Iris closes, your history, cookies, sign-ins, site data and tabs go. Cookies and site data are cleared when Iris next starts, before any page loads.

The right-click menu

  • Links: open in a new tab or in a container, Peek From 2.3, copy, Copy Clean Link From 2.3 (the address without its tracking), download.
  • Images and video: open, copy the address, save.
  • Selected text: copy, or ask Google AI Mode about it.
  • Text fields: cut, copy, paste, select all.
  • The page: back, forward, reload, select all, Reader From 2.3 on articles, and Site broken? Reload without Shields From 2.3.
