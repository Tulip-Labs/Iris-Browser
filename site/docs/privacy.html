Documentation

Privacy

Iris collects nothing. It has no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting. Tulip Labs runs no servers for Iris.

What Iris never sends

Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to Tulip Labs or to anyone on its behalf. The Privacy Report counts trackers on your own device, and keeps only tracker names and numbers.

What Iris connects to

Besides the sites you open, these are the only connections Iris makes by itself:

WhatWhereWhen
Update checkGitHub's API, for Iris's latest releaseOnce a day. It carries nothing about you beyond your network address. On Android it also downloads the new APK.
Shields listsuBlock Origin's list mirrors (ublockorigin.github.io, ublockorigin.pages.dev, cdn.jsdelivr.net)When a list is missing or more than four days old
SearchGoogleWhen you search. What you typed goes to Google, or to Google AI Mode when you ask it.
Google and YouTube warm-up From 2.3Google's and YouTube's own hostsWhen Iris starts and when you type in the address bar or the start page, at most every 20 seconds. Iris looks up the hosts' names ahead of time. From an https page you opened yourself, never a private or sandboxed tab, it also opens their first connections early.

What stays on your device

  • Your history, settings, open tabs, workspaces, cookies, sign-ins and cache are kept in your user profile, readable only by you. Each container keeps its own cookies, storage and cache.
  • Private tabs keep nothing after the last one closes.
  • With Forget everything on close From 2.3, your history, cookies, site data and tabs go when Iris closes.
  • Downloads go to your Downloads folder.

The engines

Pages are drawn by Chromium: through CEF on Linux, and through Cefrium on Android. Sites you visit receive what any browser sends them, and their own policies apply.

The full policy is in PRIVACY.md.
