Documentation

Shields

Shields stop ads and trackers inside the engine, before a request leaves your computer or phone. They are on from the first start. Click the shield in the toolbar to open the Shields panel.

How Shields work

  • Iris uses uBlock Origin's and EasyList's lists. It fetches them from uBlock Origin's mirrors every four days and turns them into rules the engine applies itself.
  • Until the lists arrive, a built-in list of ad, tracker, fingerprinting and mining domains stands in. On the first start the panel says the block list is being made ready.
  • Messaging and sign-in sites are never blocked, and neither is anything on them. This includes WhatsApp, Google accounts, Microsoft sign-in, Teams, Meet and the common captcha services.
  • Third-party cookies are refused.

Levels

LevelWhat it stops
ModerateAds and trackers from other sites (EasyList, EasyPrivacy). A site's own requests always load.
AggressiveAlso a site's own ads and trackers, uBlock Origin's filters, and the empty boxes ads leave.
Ultra aggressiveAlso cookie notices, newsletter and sign-up pop-ups and other annoyances. The built-in domain list blocks on top of the lists.

Ultra aggressive is the default From 2.3. Iris 2.3 moves everyone to it once. A level you choose after that stays. Before 2.3, the default was Aggressive.

uBlock Origin's fixes for sites the lists would otherwise break stay on at every level.

What is blocked

  • Ads and tracker requests, at the level you chose.
  • The empty boxes ads leave behind (Aggressive and up).
  • Cookie notices and annoyance pop-ups (Ultra aggressive).
  • Ads that open new tabs: a pop-up needs your click.
  • Tracking parameters in links From 2.3.

YouTube

On YouTube, the player's answer loses its ads before the player reads it. No ad is ever scheduled, and no request is blocked for YouTube to notice. YouTube's ad blocker notice is removed the same way. A feed ad that still appears is hidden, and an ad that still plays is skipped, muted. Linux and Android run the same script.

Tracking parameters From 2.3

Where Shields are up for a site, Iris removes tracking parameters before an address loads: utm_*, fbclid, gclid and others. Google's own addresses are left as they are. Copy Clean Link in the right-click menu copies an address without them.

When a site breaks

  1. Right-click the page and choose Site broken? Reload without Shields From 2.3. Iris turns Shields off for that site and reloads. Reload with Shields turns them back on.
  2. Or open the Shields panel and turn Shields off for that site, or everywhere.
  3. Or pick a lower level. Moderate never blocks a site's own requests.

If a site keeps breaking, open an issue with its address and your Shields level.

Privacy Report From 2.3

In the Shields panel, Privacy Report shows the trackers Shields stopped on this page, and today. Iris checks once when a page has finished loading, and again when you open the report. It keeps only tracker names and numbers, never the sites you visit. Private and sandboxed tabs add nothing. Requests a script makes by itself may be missing from the count.
